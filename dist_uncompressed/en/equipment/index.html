HMD (head-mounted display)

A display you wear on your head, also called a VR headset. It shows the picture right in front of your eyes and follows where your head turns and moves.

Counterweight

A weight you add to the back of the headset. Headsets are heavy at the front, so weight at the back balances them. It eases the pressure on your face and the strain on your neck.

Wireless PC VR

Playing PC VR with the picture sent from the PC to the headset over Wi-Fi. With no cable you can move freely, but a busy network can make the picture stutter or lag. On Quest you use apps such as Air Link, Steam Link or Virtual Desktop.

Wired PC VR

Playing PC VR with the headset connected to the PC by a USB cable. The picture tends to be steadier. On Quest this is Quest Link (Meta Quest Link).

Full-body tracking

Bringing not just your head and hands but also your hips, legs and feet into your avatar. You wear small devices called trackers. It lets your avatar sit, lie down or cross its legs as you do.
