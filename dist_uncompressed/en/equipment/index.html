Headset and comfort

  • Prescription lens inserts Lenses that attach inside the headset so you can see clearly without glasses. View on shop.roox.jp(opens in a new tab)
  • Face cushion (ice silk) The cushion that touches your face. Cuts down light leaking in. View on Amazon(opens in a new tab)
