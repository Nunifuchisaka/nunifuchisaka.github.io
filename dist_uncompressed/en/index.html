Products

  • Website 桜兎フルガ 公式サイト(opens in a new tab) The official website of Oto Furuga.
  • Shader DeepFrostIce An ice shader for VRChat PC avatars, with refraction, frost, freezing and melting.
  • Texture GlossySkin A glossy-skin mask for Nagi. Just swap the lilToon MatCap mask.
  • Avatar gimmicks HeartBeat A heartbeat that plays only when someone rests their head on your chest.
  • Avatar gimmicks SpiderWeb Threads stretch from your fingertips; swing your arm and threads and rose petals fall.
  • Avatar gimmicks 餓者髑髏 -Gashadokuro- A burning skeleton four times your size rises from the ground behind you and moves with you.
  • Avatar gimmick (accessory) Regret Flick the bone on your arm or leg and it flies off and stays on the floor. Touch it with the same spot to put it back.
  • Blender add-on NuniAvatarToolkit All-in-one mesh tools for avatar making: remove hidden faces, fit clothes, and more. Free.
  • Windows tool PublicWalker Lets you know when you are back in a VRChat Public instance you visited before.
  • Windows app (beta) DeskCinema Plays local videos or YouTube as a video wallpaper on each monitor.
  • Overlay app (beta) KeyDisplay An overlay that shows the keys and mouse actions you use, for Unity and Blender tutorials.
  • Windows tool ImageGetter Fetches and saves the share images (og:image) of many URLs at once.
