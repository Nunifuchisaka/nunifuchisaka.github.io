Part 3Building gimmicks yourself with the Animator

From how the Animator works to toggles, multi-step and gradual changes, expressions, touch reactions, and what to check when a gimmick doesn't work. Read in this order and you'll be able to build gimmicks on your own.

  1. #6 How the Animator works What happens between pressing a menu item and your avatar changing. The roles of layers like FX, states, transitions, and parameters. November 3, 2026
  2. #7 Making animations A gimmick's visual change is written in an animation clip. How to record one, what you can animate, and how to fix broken paths. November 3, 2026
  3. #8 Building a menu toggle Build a gimmick that shows and hides an accessory from the menu, starting from the Animator. Parameters, the menu, and hooking it up to your avatar. November 3, 2026
  4. #9 Switching in steps and changing gradually Choose one of several outfits (Int), or turn a dial to change color or size (Float and Radial Puppet). How to use Motion Time and Blend Trees. November 3, 2026
  5. #10 Changing expressions with hand gestures Switch facial expressions with the GestureLeft and GestureRight numbers. Which hand wins, overlap with blinking and lip sync, and using grip strength. November 3, 2026
  6. #11 Gimmicks that react to touch Blush when your head is patted, change expression when your hair is grabbed. Contacts and PhysBone parameters tell the Animator you were touched. November 3, 2026
  7. #12 When a gimmick doesn't work Nothing happens from the menu, only you can see it, it resets when you change worlds. The order to check things, and a list of common causes. November 3, 2026

Part 4When it doesn't look the way you expected

Why your avatar looks different in Unity and VRChat, and the draw order of transparent parts.

  1. #13 It looks different in Unity and VRChat Expressions or body shapes you set in Unity snap back in VRChat. Usually it's because the FX Animator is overwriting the BlendShapes. November 3, 2026
