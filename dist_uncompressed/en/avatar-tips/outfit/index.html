Avatar Tips #3

Dressing your avatar

This tip shows you how to put an outfit you bought on your avatar. Today, with Modular Avatar, most outfits go on in a few clicks. It also covers what to do when your body pokes through the clothes, and how to turn the outfit on and off from a menu, with recreated views and a figure you can play with.

Published November 3, 2026 · This is about VRChat avatars (Avatars 3.0) and Modular Avatar. Current as of November 2026.

Read this when

  • You want to put an outfit you bought on BOOTH or elsewhere on your own avatar.
  • You put on the outfit, but it doesn't follow your body when you move. Or your body pokes through the clothes.
  • You want to put the outfit on and take it off from a menu.

Check before you start

Every outfit is made for certain avatars. Check the manual or the store page.

Supported avatars
If your avatar is named on the store page as a supported avatar, the outfit is made to fit it. You can put it on almost as is with the steps on this page.
Unsupported avatars
You can sometimes put an outfit on an avatar that isn't listed, but you have to adjust the size and shape yourself. This is hard, so start with outfits made for your avatar.
Required shaders
Many outfits use a shader such as lilToon. Add the shader named in the manual to your project first.
Terms of use
What you may edit, and the rules for uploading, are different for each outfit. Read them before you use it.

Why use Modular Avatar

In the past, you dressed an avatar by moving the outfit's bones into the avatar's bones one by one. It took time, mistakes were hard to undo, and you had to do it all again when the avatar or the outfit was updated.

Modular Avatar merges the outfit's bones into the avatar's bones automatically, only when you upload or press Play in Unity (Merge Armature). The avatar and outfit you edit stay as they are, so nothing gets broken. To remove the outfit, just delete it from the Hierarchy.

Add Modular Avatar

Add Modular Avatar to your project with VCC or ALCOM. How to install VCC and ALCOM is covered in #1.

  1. On the Modular Avatar website (opens in a new tab), go to "Installation". If you use VCC, press "Add Modular Avatar to VCC". If you use ALCOM, enter https://vpm.nadena.dev/vpm.json under "Add VPM Repository".
  2. Open your project with "Manage Project" in VCC or "Manage" in ALCOM, and press the + next to Modular Avatar to add it.

Steps to dress your avatar

  1. Drag the outfit's unitypackage into Unity's Project window and press "Import".
  2. In the outfit's folder, find the prefab with your avatar's name. Outfits often have a separate prefab for each supported avatar.
  3. Drag that prefab onto your avatar in the Hierarchy (the object with the VRC Avatar Descriptor) to make it a child of the avatar.
  4. Right-click the outfit and choose [ModularAvatar] Setup Outfit.
  5. Turn off the clothes the avatar was wearing (uncheck the box at the top left of the Inspector).
  6. Press Unity's Play button, and check that the clothes follow the avatar when it moves.
  1. Put the outfit's prefab directly under the avatar (as its child). Don't put it outside the avatar or inside the Armature.
  2. Right-click the outfit and choose this. It finds the Armature inside the outfit and adds Merge Armature to it.

What Setup Outfit does

When you choose Setup Outfit, MA Merge Armature is added to the Armature inside the outfit, and MA Outfit Root is added to the top of the outfit.

Merge Armature finds the outfit's bones by name and merges them into the avatar's bones with the same names. Bones the avatar doesn't have (such as skirt bones) are added as children of the avatar's bones. The outfit's swaying parts (PhysBones) are also fixed so they move the bones they were merged into.

The merge happens only when you press Play and when you upload. When you stop playing, the Hierarchy goes back to how it was.

  1. Where the outfit's bones are merged. The avatar's Armature goes here. Setup Outfit fills it in for you, so you normally don't change it.
  2. Makes the outfit follow the avatar's pose while you edit. Setup Outfit sets it to this one-way mode.
  3. Renames the bones the avatar doesn't have, so they don't clash with bones from other items.

When your body pokes through the clothes

If the body is bigger than the clothes, your skin pokes through them when you move. Many avatars have BlendShapes (shape keys) that shrink or hide the body under the clothes. They have names like Shrink_... or Hide_..., and they are listed in the avatar's manual.

Raise the value of that BlendShape on Body's Skinned Mesh Renderer to shrink it. But the value stays the same even when you take the clothes off. Also, if the avatar's FX Animator writes to this BlendShape, VRChat changes it back from the value you set in Unity (#13).

We recommend adding MA Shape Changer inside the outfit. It sets the body's BlendShape to a value you choose only while the outfit is on. When you turn the outfit off, the body goes back to normal. With Delete, it can also remove the body's polygons under the clothes to make the avatar lighter.

  1. Put in the body mesh you want to shrink (usually Body).
  2. Set uses this value while the outfit is on. Use it for BlendShapes that shrink the body.
  3. Delete removes the part this BlendShape affects while the outfit is on. Use it for parts the clothes hide completely.

How to shrink

A value set on Body stays the same whether the clothes are on or off.

With clothes on: the body pokes through the clothes. Raise the value.

With clothes off: the body goes back to its original shape.

Put clothes on and off from the menu

With Modular Avatar, you can add an outfit on/off switch to the Expressions Menu (Action Menu) without building an Animator yourself.

  1. Right-click the avatar in the Hierarchy and choose Modular Avatar → Create Toggle. An object with Menu Item, Menu Installer and Object Toggle is created as a child of the avatar.
  2. Rename the object to the name you want in the menu (for example, "Dress"). This name becomes the menu item's name.
  3. Press + in Object Toggle and drag the outfit object in. With the checkbox on the right checked, it turns on when selected. Unchecked, it turns off when selected.
  4. If the avatar wears these clothes from the start, turn on "Is Default" in Menu Item so it starts on.
  5. The Shape Changer inside the outfit follows the outfit's on/off state. When you take the outfit off, the body goes back to normal.

To build the Animator yourself and make the on/off logic from scratch, see #8 Building a menu toggle.

Test it in Play mode

  • When you press Unity's Play button, Modular Avatar merges the outfit. Move the avatar's pose in the Scene and check that the clothes follow and nothing pokes through.
  • If you install Gesture Manager, you can open the Expressions Menu during Play and test the outfit on/off too.
  • If it all looks good, upload and check in VRChat as well. In front of a mirror, try big moves like crouching and raising your arms.

Common problems

The clothes don't follow

What happens
When you press Play or move in VRChat, the clothes stay where they are.
Cause
You haven't run Setup Outfit, or the outfit isn't a child of the avatar.
Fix
Put the outfit directly under the avatar, then right-click it and run Setup Outfit again. Also check that MA Merge Armature is on the outfit's Armature.

The body pokes through the clothes

What happens
When you move, the skin on the chest, belly or thighs shows outside the clothes.
Cause
The body under the clothes is still at full size.
Fix
Add the BlendShape that shrinks the body to the outfit with MA Shape Changer. If you set the value directly on Body, also check that the FX Animator doesn't write to it (#13).

The clothes float or don't fit

What happens
The clothes float away from the body. The sleeves or hem are the wrong length.
Cause
You are using a prefab for a different avatar, or you changed your avatar's body shape.
Fix
Check that the prefab is for your avatar. If you changed the body shape with BlendShapes, set the same BlendShapes on the outfit, or use MA Blendshape Sync to keep them at the same value.

Swaying parts are applied twice

What happens
The chest or hair sways strangely. It bounces or shakes.
Cause
The outfit and the avatar may both have PhysBones that move the same bones.
Fix
Read the outfit's manual, decide which PhysBones to use, and delete or turn off the ones you don't need.

The menu doesn't show up

What happens
The toggle you made doesn't appear in the Expressions Menu.
Cause
There is no Menu Installer, or the toggle object is outside the avatar. The menu may also be full.
Fix
Put the toggle under the avatar as its child, and check that it has a Menu Installer. Also test it in Play mode with Gesture Manager.

The clothes turn pink

What happens
The outfit's clothes show up in solid pink.
Cause
The shader the outfit uses isn't in your project.
Fix
Add the shader named in the manual (such as lilToon). If it still doesn't work, choose the shader on the material again.

You want to put the outfit on an unsupported avatar

What happens
An outfit you like doesn't support your avatar.
Cause
The body shape and bone positions are different, so it doesn't fit as is.
Fix
First line it up roughly with "Reset position to base avatar" on the Outfit Root, then fix the bone positions and sizes bit by bit. There are also tools to help you fit it. This takes time, so try it after you are used to editing avatars. Also check that the terms of use allow edits for unsupported avatars.

Things to watch out for

  • You don't need to unpack the outfit or avatar prefabs. Leaving them packed makes updates easier.
  • Turn off the original clothes instead of deleting them, so you can bring them back later.
  • Adding an outfit increases polygons and materials, and can lower your performance rank. Check it before you upload.
  • Back up your project before you edit, just to be safe.

Summary

  • Put the prefab made for your avatar under the avatar as its child.
  • Right-click and choose Setup Outfit, and Modular Avatar merges the bones automatically. Your original data isn't broken.
  • To stop the body poking through, use MA Shape Changer to shrink the body only while the outfit is on.
  • With Create Toggle, you can put the clothes on and off from the menu.
